SZ / PROFILE

Researcher.
Engineer. Creator.

I am Shuyan Zhang, a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Warwick, where I joined the Ubiquitous & Visual Computing Lab (UVLab) in September 2026.

My interests span robotics, machine learning and computer vision. I completed my MSc in Robotics at the University of Manchester in September 2025, graduating with Distinction.

Alongside research and engineering, I make illustrations and visual work, drawing on two years of experience in media operations, video editing and design.
